ABOUT ELECTROLIX

Electrical Guides That Answer Practical Questions

Electrical products are not interchangeable. Differences in ratings, construction and intended use can affect performance and suitability. Electrolix explains these details in straightforward guides, helping readers understand a product before shortlisting it.

Our resources are useful for homeowners planning improvements, electricians sourcing materials and professionals reviewing equipment for a project. The aim is to make technical information easier to follow—not to replace product documentation or advice from a qualified electrical professional.
