BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Start with the specification, not the sales pitch.

The right product depends on the job, the system and the conditions of use. Use these points as a starting checklist, then confirm technical decisions with the relevant documentation or a qualified professional.

01
Rated capacity

Check voltage, current, load and other ratings relevant to the application.

02
Compatibility

Confirm that the product suits the existing system, accessories and installation method.

03
Standards and protection

Review applicable standards, protection features and the manufacturer's instructions.

04
Operating environment

Consider temperature, moisture, dust, enclosure and expected operating conditions.

05
Installation and upkeep

Account for installation space, accessibility, maintenance and service requirements.

06
Total suitability

Compare specifications and long-term suitability rather than price in isolation.
