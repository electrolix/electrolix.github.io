ABOUT ELECTROLIX

Electrical Information for Everyday and Professional Decisions

Electrical products can look similar while serving different purposes. Their ratings, construction, compatibility and installation requirements all affect whether they are suitable for a particular job. Electrolix explains these differences in clear, useful language.

The site is for homeowners researching an upgrade, electricians selecting materials, and professionals who need a reliable starting point when reviewing electrical equipment. Each guide is intended to help readers understand the subject before they make a selection.
