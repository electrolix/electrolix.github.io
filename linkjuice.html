What is a 16 Amp Switch?

A 16 Amp switch is a heavy-duty electrical switch designed to control appliances that draw higher current than standard lighting circuits. It is commonly used for air conditioners, water heaters, microwave ovens, induction cooktops and other high-power equipment. Unlike a standard 6 Amp switch, a 16 Amp switch is built using stronger contacts and durable materials that safely handle higher electrical loads. Choosing the correct switch improves electrical safety, reduces overheating and ensures reliable long-term performance.

Common Applications

Air Conditioners

Suitable for split and window AC units.

Water Heaters

Handles higher current safely.

Kitchen Appliances

Ideal for microwave ovens and induction cooktops.

Commercial Equipment

Used in offices and industrial installations.
