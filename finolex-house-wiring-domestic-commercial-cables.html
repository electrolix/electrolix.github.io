There is no single Finolex wire that is universally correct for every house-wiring circuit. The suitable product depends on the circuit load, conductor size, insulation category, installation method, protection and applicable requirements. Review the exact Finolex product specification and have the final selection confirmed by a qualified electrician.

Eleczo's Finolex house-wiring category lists several sizes, including 0.75 sq mm, 1 sq mm, 1.5 sq mm, 4 sq mm and 6 sq mm, as well as other sizes. Availability can vary by product category, so check the current product listing for the required size.

FR stands for Flame Retardant. FRLS means Flame Retardant Low Smoke, adding a low-smoke characteristic under specified test conditions. FR-LSH means Flame Retardant Low Smoke Halogen, with additional requirements relating to smoke and halogen-related fire performance. Always verify the exact manufacturer specification before selecting a product.

Finolex house-wire products can be relevant to commercial building applications, but suitability depends on the project design. Commercial installations can involve more complex distribution, longer routes, higher demand and additional requirements, so the selected product should be confirmed against the electrical design.

Start with the circuit load and then assess installation method, route length, grouping, ambient conditions, voltage drop, protective-device coordination and applicable standards. Do not select a wire size solely from a general appliance or room-based rule.
