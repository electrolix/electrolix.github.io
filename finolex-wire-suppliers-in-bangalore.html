Finolex offers wires across categories such as FR, FRLS, and FRLSH insulation types, in a range of conductor sizes, intended for house wiring, lighting, power sockets, and industrial use.

Finolex's house wiring range covers multiple sizes and insulation types designed for domestic and commercial buildings. The exact product depends on the specific circuit and load involved.

Wire size should be based on the load, circuit length, installation method, voltage drop, ambient conditions, applicable standards, and the rating of the protection device on that circuit — not on the appliance type alone.

FR refers to flame-retardant insulation. FRLS adds low-smoke properties on top of flame retardance. FRLSH further incorporates properties associated with reduced halogen content. The specific performance characteristics of any product should be verified from official documentation.

Finolex's lighting wire range is generally available in smaller conductor sizes suited to the lower current typically drawn by lighting circuits, though the final size should be confirmed against the actual circuit design.
