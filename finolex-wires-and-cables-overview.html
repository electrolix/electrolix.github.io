Types of Finolex Wires and Cables

FR Wires

FR stands for flame-retardant. These wires are designed with flame-retardant characteristics specified for the product and may be used in suitable building-wire applications.

FRLS Wires

FRLS refers to flame-retardant, low-smoke characteristics. These properties can be relevant in installations where both flame behaviour and smoke emission are considered.

FRLSH Wires

FRLSH commonly identifies flame-retardant, low-smoke and low-halogen characteristics. The exact performance should always be confirmed from the product's technical documentation.

Copper Wires

Copper is widely used for electrical conductors because of its electrical conductivity and practical mechanical characteristics. Conductor size still needs to match the circuit design.

House Wiring, Domestic and Commercial Cables

Building-wire products can be used for fixed domestic and commercial circuits where the product's rating and construction suit the installation.

Industrial Cables

Industrial cables can be intended for power, control or other industrial applications where electrical, mechanical and environmental requirements may differ from ordinary building wiring.

Finolex Wire Sizes and Applications

Common Finolex wire searches include 0.75 sq mm, 1 sq mm, 1.5 sq mm, 4 sq mm and 6 sq mm. Wire size is normally expressed as the conductor's cross-sectional area in square millimetres.

A larger cross-sectional area can generally support a higher current under comparable conditions, but wire size should never be selected from size alone. Electrical load, installation method, ambient temperature, grouping, permissible voltage drop and applicable requirements can affect the final selection.
