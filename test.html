1. Introduction

Electrolix ("we," "our," "the website," or "Electrolix") is an informational website dedicated to electrical products, electrical components, and electrical industry-related content. We publish educational articles, product guides, comparisons, specifications, and general buying-related information covering items such as switches, sockets, cover plates, electrical modules, modular boxes, metal boxes, fans, lighting products, wires and cables, fan regulators, and other electrical accessories.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use Electrolix. By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Electrolix does not require visitors to create an account, register, or log in to access any content on the website. You can freely browse and read our articles, guides, and product information without providing any personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through account creation, registration forms, or login systems, because no such systems exist on this website.

That said, like most websites, certain standard technical information may be processed automatically in the course of operating the site. This can include information handled by our web hosting provider, servers, security systems, analytics tools, cookies, or similar technologies, where such services are used. This may include details such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and similar technical data. This happens as a normal part of how websites function and does not involve you actively submitting personal information.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website may place on your device to help the website function properly, remember preferences, or gather usage information.

Electrolix may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as basic website functionality, performance, security, analytics, or improving the overall user experience, where applicable. We do not claim to use cookies beyond what is actually implemented on the website.

You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how some parts of the website function.

4. Third-Party Services

Electrolix may use third-party services to help operate, secure, and improve the website. These may include services such as web hosting providers, analytics services, security services, or advertising services, where applicable.

Any such third-party service may process limited technical information in accordance with its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services if you would like more information about how they handle data. We do not list specific third-party tools here unless they are confirmed to be in active use on the website.
