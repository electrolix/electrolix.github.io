FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about electrical products and installation.

An electrical fan regulator is a device used to control the speed of a ceiling or wall fan by regulating the power supplied to the fan.

A fan regulator controls the electrical power delivered to the fan motor, allowing you to increase or decrease the fan speed.

The best regulator depends on your fan type, required speed control, compatibility, design preference and electrical specifications.

Electrical installation should be performed by a qualified electrician, especially when working with mains electricity.
