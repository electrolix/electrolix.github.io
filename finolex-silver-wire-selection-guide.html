Choosing the Right Wire Size

There is no single wire size that is correct for every electrical application. Size selection should be based on the complete electrical design and applicable requirements rather than a rule of thumb or a previous installation.

Key factors include:

  • Electrical load and expected current
  • Circuit length and voltage-drop considerations where relevant
  • Installation method and routing
  • Circuit protection and overall design
  • Conductor characteristics
  • Insulation requirements
  • Ambient temperature and environmental conditions
  • Grouping, enclosure and other installation factors
  • Applicable electrical standards and local requirements

Do not prescribe a wire size simply because a particular appliance or room is involved. Where safety, load calculations or compliance are important, the installation should be assessed by a qualified electrician or electrical professional.
