What Is a 6 Module Cover Plate?

A 6 module cover plate is the visible front plate fitted over a compatible modular switchboard arrangement with space for six modules. It provides a neat, finished appearance around switches, sockets and other modular accessories while helping keep the installation organised. The exact combination of fittings depends on the module sizes and the layout supported by the selected switch series.

Where Are Six-Module Plates Used?

A six-module layout is useful when a switchboard needs to accommodate a combination of modular controls and accessories in one location. It may be considered for residential rooms, kitchens, offices, shops and other spaces where the planned electrical points can be arranged within a six-module configuration. Plan the switch and socket positions with a qualified electrician before choosing the plate.

What to Check Before Buying a 6 Module Cover Plate

  • Brand and series compatibility: Match the cover plate with the same modular range as the switches, sockets and support frame. Similar module counts do not guarantee that products from different series will fit together.
  • Module layout: Confirm that the six-module arrangement suits the number and type of mechanisms you intend to install.
  • Support or fixing frame: Read the listing carefully to see whether the frame is included or needs to be purchased separately.
  • Material and finish: Select a finish and colour that suit the room and the surrounding switchgear. Check the product specifications for the actual material.
  • Mounting and dimensions: Verify the required mounting arrangement and dimensions against the existing electrical box and installation.

Materials, Colours and Finishes

Six-module cover plates are available in different styles and finishes, depending on the brand and product range. Common choices include simple white plates, darker neutral shades and decorative finishes such as metallic or wood-look designs. The material, surface finish and included components can vary, so use the product specifications rather than appearance alone when comparing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 6 module cover plate?

A 6 module cover plate is the front plate used with a compatible modular switchboard arrangement designed to accommodate six module spaces.

Will any 6 module cover plate fit my switches?

Not necessarily. Check that the plate belongs to the same brand and product series as the mechanisms and support frame, and confirm the required module layout before ordering.

Does a 6 module cover plate include a support frame?

It depends on the product. Some listings include a support or fixing frame, while others are supplied as a cover plate only. Check the product description before purchase.

What should I check before buying a 6 module cover plate?

Check brand and series compatibility, module arrangement, dimensions, frame inclusion, mounting type, material, colour and finish.
