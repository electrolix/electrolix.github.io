It is a building wire from Polycab's house-wire portfolio, generally used for electrical distribution and connections within buildings. The exact conductor, insulation and performance characteristics depend on the product range and variant.

They are commonly considered for residential building wiring and, where the product specification is suitable, offices, shops and other commercial buildings. Applications can include lighting, sockets, distribution boards and suitable appliance circuits.

Some Polycab house-wire products are designed for commercial building applications. Suitability depends on the specific product, circuit load, installation conditions and project requirements.

Wire size should be selected from the electrical load and installation design, taking account of circuit length, installation method, protective devices, environmental conditions and applicable requirements. A qualified electrician or electrical professional should be consulted where appropriate.

Conductor size, product series, insulation type, coil length, configuration, supplier pricing, market conditions and availability can all affect the price.

The basic principles are similar, but commercial installations can involve more circuits, varied loads, larger distribution systems and different planning or operating conditions. The selected wire must match the actual application.

Consult a qualified electrician whenever the installation involves new wiring, significant load changes, distribution-board work, uncertain conductor sizing or conditions that require professional electrical design and testing.
