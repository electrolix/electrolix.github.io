Key Features and Characteristics to Consider

The most useful way to evaluate an electrical wire is to focus on the characteristics that affect its suitability for the intended installation. Product-specific details should always be checked rather than assumed.

Conductor construction

The conductor is the part of the wire that carries current. Its material, construction and cross-sectional area can influence how the product is selected for a circuit. Verify the exact conductor information provided for the product instead of assuming that every size or variant has identical construction.

Wire size

Wire size is a key selection factor because the required size depends on the circuit and installation conditions. A larger size is not automatically the right answer, and a smaller size should not be chosen simply because it is commonly used elsewhere. The size should match the requirements established for the electrical installation.

Insulation

Insulation is essential to the overall design of an electrical wire. However, the exact insulation type and its documented properties should be checked for the specific product. Do not assume that every wire in a broad category has the same performance characteristics.

Ratings and documentation

Where voltage ratings, temperature-related limits, standards, markings or other technical information are relevant, verify them using reliable product documentation. The appropriate selection depends on the application, so the applicable rating should be confirmed before installation.
