Finolex FR wire is a PVC-insulated electrical wire manufactured with a flame-retardant compound, designed to resist the spread of flame along the cable. It is used for power and lighting wiring in homes, offices, and light industrial settings, typically at a 1100 V voltage grade under IS 694:2010.

Yes. FR wires are the commonly used category for residential wiring in India today, covering lighting circuits, power sockets, and general household electrical points. The right size still depends on your specific load and circuit design, so it's best to confirm this with an electrician.

FR wires are flame-retardant. FRLS and FR-LSH wires add reduced smoke and, in the case of FR-LSH, reduced halogen gas emission during combustion. These are often preferred in enclosed or densely occupied spaces where smoke inhalation risk is a greater concern.

It depends on the circuit. Smaller sizes, such as 1 sq mm or 1.5 sq mm, are commonly used for lighting, while larger sizes, such as 4 sq mm or 6 sq mm, are used for power sockets and higher-load appliances. A qualified electrician can calculate the exact size based on your connected load and wiring length.

Wire size, coil length, copper price movements, the specific variant (FR, FR-LSH, Gold, or Silver), and the seller all influence the final price. It's best to check current pricing on a retailer's product page rather than relying on a fixed figure.
